About the study

This is the corresponding website to the publication Colocalization of fibrosis and inflammation in the dura in progressive multiple sclerosis by Börsch & Schafflick et al.

We present a large scale single cell transcriptional and spatial atlas of human dura in health and disease (multiple sclerosis). The interactive atlas brings together single-nucleus and spatial transcriptomic data (Xenium).

The raw sequencing data are available in GEO.
